Services

Three specialised areas that build on each other: from certified energy monitoring to automation, and from there to smart energy management.

EcoXpert Power Management

EcoXpert partner of Schneider Electric since 2018, at Certified level, specialising in energy management and monitoring solutions.

We integrate EcoStruxure™ Power Monitoring Expert (PME) — Schneider Electric's software designed for large, critical facilities — to monitor and analyse electrical installations, centralising consumption data, electrical parameters, alarms, events and power quality.

Our experience spans from design and integration through to configuration and commissioning of the solution, whether on-site or cloud-based.

We turn your electrical installation's data into useful information to improve its efficiency, reliability and operation.

Energy Management Systems

Building on our knowledge of power systems and their integration with plant automation, we optimise plant operation with ETAP Real-Time.

Real-time energy management

Since 2011 we have been an ETAP System Integrator Partner, reaching Select level in 2016 for real-time energy management solutions with ETAP Real-Time. Our collaboration with Operation Technology Inc. (ETAP) since 2012 has enabled us to take part in implementing advanced energy management systems for industrial and infrastructure facilities.

We also carry out integrations in electrical systems that require control with EcoStruxure™ Power Operation.

We integrate ETAP Real-Time's Power Management, Energy Management and Intelligent Load Shedding solutions:

  • Power System Monitoring & Simulation (PSMS)
  • Intelligent Load Shedding (ILS)
  • Energy Management System (EMS)

Simulation and predictive analysis

It's estimated that a third of blackouts in industrial power systems are attributed to operator error. Our energy management system, based on ETAP Real-Time, helps anticipate them:

  • Simulation of breaker operations
  • Identification of potential operational issues
  • Prediction of system response to possible operator actions
  • Operator training and assistance

What we do

Specialised services across three areas

ETIC Telecom Specialist Distributor

  • Since 2017 we have been specialist distributors of ETIC Telecom in Spain, offering industrial communication solutions for connectivity and remote access.
  • We supply industrial IPL/SIG routers, RAS solutions for remote machine access, and SHDSL and SPE switches, enabling long-distance communications over two-wire cabling.
  • We also develop specific solutions based on this equipment, extending its capabilities for particular industrial applications.
See details →

Automation, Control & Communications

  • Since 2010 we have supported systems integrators and engineering teams with specialised PLC programming and SCADA expertise. We work as an extension of our clients' teams, on either a one-off or ongoing basis.
  • Consulting to define automation, communications, control and supervision systems
  • Proven experience implementing Schneider Electric/Aveva software such as Unity Pro / Control Expert / Machine Expert / AVEVA Edge / Citect / Plant SCADA / Aveva Reports for Operations / Vijeo Designer
  • Custom software development when a project requires it
See details →

Energy Management Systems

  • Since 2011 we have been ETAP partners for implementing energy management solutions based on ETAP Real-Time
  • Since 2018 we have been EcoXpert partners of Schneider Electric, implementing and maintaining PME solutions
  • We also carry out integrations in electrical systems that require control via EcoStruxure™ Power Operation
  • Real-time electrical plant control with ETAP Real-Time
  • Predictive simulation of possible events
  • Intelligent load shedding to minimise plant downtime
  • Economic dispatch and automatic generator control

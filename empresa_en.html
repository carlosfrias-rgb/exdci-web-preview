eXDCi Solutions

An engineering company born from persistence and technical knowledge, not from chance.

The initial idea

We were founded with a clear vision: to adapt to market evolution and the real needs of each project. We combine our technical knowledge with a network of leading partners in our areas of specialisation to deliver reliable, flexible engineering solutions tailored to each client.

The constant evolution of market needs has led us to build a collaborative model with large companies, providing flexible engineering services tailored to the needs of each project.

Our goal is to achieve the satisfaction of both the client and the contracting party, by delivering quality solutions that meet the requirements, objectives and deadlines set for each project.

Who we are

From that idea and those values, eXDCi Solutions was born at the end of 2010: a full-service engineering company that continuously pursues standardisation of its processes and the optimal use of the specific tools each project requires.

We provide advisory, engineering, project management and control/energy management systems integration services for the industrial and infrastructure sectors. We also supply industrial communication equipment, enabling complete integration solutions — from equipment supply through to system commissioning.

Our methodology places engineering at the forefront, as the essential foundation throughout the life of the project.

Based at the Enertic building

Our headquarters are in the Enertic building, in San Sebastián (Gipuzkoa, Spain) — a hub for renewable energy, energy management and Smart energy companies.

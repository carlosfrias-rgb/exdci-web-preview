eXDCi Solutions

An engineering company born from persistence and technical knowledge, not from chance.

The initial idea

The idea was born from a vision of the market's continuous evolution and the strategy of large companies: to build an engineering services company adapted to the real requirements of each project.

The pace of change in market needs has consolidated a collaborative way of working with larger organisations, offering them dynamic services tailored to each project.

The goal is the satisfaction of both the contracting party and the end client, through a quality product that meets requirements and the deadlines set for each project.

Who we are

From that idea and those values, eXDCi Solutions was born at the end of 2010: a full-service engineering company that continuously pursues standardisation of its processes and the optimal use of the specific tools each project requires.

We provide advisory, engineering, project management and control/energy management systems integration services, in the infrastructure and industrial sectors.

Our methodology places engineering at the forefront, as the essential foundation throughout the life of the project.

Based at the Enertic building

Our headquarters are in the Enertic building, in San Sebastián (Gipuzkoa, Spain) — a hub for renewable energy, energy management and smart energy companies.
